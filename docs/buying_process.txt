How buying a house works with us
Step 1: Choose a house and pay 10% token money to reserve it for 30 days.
 
Step 2: Submit copies of the buyer's CNIC and two passport-size photos.
 
Step 3: Pay the remaining amount at the time of transfer. The transfer usually takes 2 to 4 weeks.
 
Our office is open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm. We are closed on Sundays.
